About Daniel

Fifteen years, ten world records.

Daniel Cutting is a football freestyler, YouTuber and entertainer. A competitive freestyle career became a performance craft, and then a channel followed by millions of people around the world.

Today the work spans three things: live shows and appearances, content across YouTube, TikTok and Instagram, and campaigns for brands. Joshua now has a channel and an audience of his own, and the videos they make together are the most-watched on the channel.
